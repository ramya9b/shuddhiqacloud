2. What Shuddhi QA Is

Shuddhi QA is a free, AI-powered test case generator for QA engineers and software testers. It generates structured test cases from requirement documents, business flow descriptions, Jira tickets, and Azure DevOps work items. Shuddhi QA includes a Smart Detect feature that automatically identifies the enterprise platform, business domain, compliance obligations, and stakeholders from your requirement text.

Account & Usage Limits

  • Anonymous use (no sign-in): 3 free generations per browser. No account required.
  • Signed-in use: Sign in with Google or Microsoft to get 10 free generations. Your platform preferences sync across devices via Firebase Firestore.
  • Account deletion: Click your avatar → Delete my data & account inside the app at any time. This permanently deletes all your synced preferences and your Firebase Auth account.
  • Data ownership: Signing in does not transfer ownership of your content. See our Privacy Policy for full details of what is stored.
  • AI generation powered by Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq (Llama), OpenAI GPT-4o, and Together AI (DeepSeek V3) — using shared or your own API keys
  • Exports to Excel, PDF, Markdown, Jira Zephyr CSV, TestRail, Gherkin, Playwright, and more
  • Direct push to Azure DevOps Test Plans
  • Google Cloud Billing sync (read-only)
  • All data stored locally in your browser — no server-side data retention
