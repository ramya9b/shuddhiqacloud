Quality

Why the output is actually usable

  • Clarification gate — asks smart questions when a requirement is too thin
  • ≥10 numbered steps where realistic — never one-liners
  • Zero generic results — every expected result is specific and assertable
  • No duplicates — happy path, negative, boundary, role, data & integration
  • Zero hallucination — flags [needs confirmation] instead of inventing fields
Trust

Privacy by design — your keys, your data

  • API keys live only in your browser's localStorage
  • Requests pass through a lightweight proxy that forwards to your provider —
    never logs or stores your keys or the requirements you paste in
  • Azure OpenAI runs in your own Azure region for data residency
  • No tracking cookies. Generated test cases never leave your browser.
